About Me

Turning data into meaningful business decisions

Turning Data Into Decisions.


I'm a Data Analyst focused on transforming raw data into meaningful insights that help businesses understand performance, identify opportunities, and make better decisions. My core toolkit includes Excel, Power BI, SQL, Power Query, DAX, and data visualization, with hands-on experience building analytical reports and interactive dashboards.


I approach every dataset with three questions:
What happened? → Why did it happen? → What can we do next?


Focus: Data Analysis | Business Intelligence | Power BI | SQL | Excel | Data Visualization


What drives me is solving problems that don't have an obvious first step. I enjoy breaking a messy dataset down, testing assumptions, and building toward a solution that's both simple and reliable.


With 5 years of experience in the call center industry as a Senior Training Specialist, I've been responsible for producing the company's performance reports — turning raw operational data into insights that shaped training decisions and improved team performance. This experience taught me how to translate numbers into actions that actually matter to a business, long before I formally studied data analysis.


I'm a continuous learner at heart, always looking to sharpen my analytical skills and stay close to real-world data problems. I believe well-designed data systems can genuinely change how teams make decisions.
